# Tajada Studios

Website for Tajada Studios, home of Historias de Pasta. Static, bilingual (EN/ES), served with GitHub Pages at tajadastudios.com.

## Pages
- `/` and `/es/`: home (channels, behind the studio, contact)
- `/about/` and `/es/sobre/`: Kiko's story
- `/legal/` and `/es/legal/`: legal notice and privacy
- `404.html`: branded not-found page (both languages)

Each English page links to its Spanish twin with `hreflang`, so Google indexes both. The language switch remembers the choice in the browser; a first visit from a Spanish browser goes to `/es/`.

## Brand
- Mark: the half slice (watermelon). Green rind on light backgrounds, cream rind on green.
- Colours: rind green `#1F5C4A`, melon coral `#F2664A`, cocoa `#241A12`, cream `#FFF6EA`.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque, self-hosted in `fonts/` (no Google Fonts request).
- Styles live in `css/site.css`. Share images: `og-en.png`, `og-es.png` (1200×630).
