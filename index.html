Where it starts

Zaragoza first, then Madrid for an M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering at the Universidad Politécnica. Maths, statistics and systems: a way of thinking I still use every day.

What I do

From internships at Dematic and Biogen to adidas, where I was Chief of Staff for global product supply and logistics. Today I'm Senior Manager for Digital Process Management, bridging SAP planning tools and the people who use them.

Why Tajada

Money is easier to understand as a story, with real numbers. That idea became Historias de Pasta. I'm also a CFA® Level III candidate and a marathon runner. Tajada Studios is where these projects live.
